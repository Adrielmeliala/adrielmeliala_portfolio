AI & Automation | 2026 | Research paper, Team project

Android Log Analysis using LLM

Solution Pipeline

A pipeline of adaptive buffering, regex parsing with token masking, TF-IDF/SVD/UMAP/DBSCAN clustering, and an LLaMA agent that produces structured summaries.

My Role

Led the problem brainstorming, shaped the LLM-based analysis solution, and contributed to writing the research paper.
